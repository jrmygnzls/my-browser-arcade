Welcome to the Frontier

Your map begins as a completely open field. Build towers to create a maze that forces enemy creeps through your kill zones. Every normal tower blocks one grid tile, and the route finder checks every placement before allowing it.

Pathstone (available from the start): this special structure creates a mandatory checkpoint. Creeps must pass through its tile before heading to the exit. It does not block movement, and only one can be active at a time.

Start with Arcane Sentries, Frost Sigils, Venom Spires, and Siege Cannons. More complex towers unlock as you reach higher waves. Upgrade towers and choose a specialization at level 2.
